Getting closer to summer and lots going on!

**Reading:** For philosophy I’ve picked up two new books I’m excited about: The [*Revenge of Reason* by Peter Wolfendale](https://www.urbanomic.com/book/the-revenge-of-reason/); [*The Sovereignty of Good* by Iris Murdoch](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/11232.The_Sovereignty_of_Good). These are in preparation for an epistemological essay series I’d like to write about reason as the *act* of reasoning. For some semi-lighter stuff, I’ve got [*The Far Call* by Gordon R. Dickson](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/1549732.The_Far_Call) as inspiration for some world-building I’m doing.

**Making:** The new website! The new TTRPG! (see the [TTRPG page](/ttrpg/)) Which is in its first play-test. I’m testing my new process for the large print photograph; I'll post it when there's enough to report back on.

**Next:** More posting through May as I return to online life and support the new TTRPG. Also turning my hand to essay writing again, and starting the new novella in earnest.

If you want to know when something new goes up here, the [RSS feed](/feed.xml) is the best way. No newsletter from here as I'm trying to keep my own inbox quieter and I won't add to anyone else's. For notifications you can find me on [Substack](https://maxfieldjweir.substack.com) and Bluesky.

Thanks for reading!
