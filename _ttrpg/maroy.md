# Purpose of Product
* Provide a four act adventure narrative set in 1935, inspired by the culture of the Faroe Islands and isolation, alongside similar games like *Call of Cthulhu: Masks of Nyarlathotep* and *Silent Hill*
* Offer fans of historical cosmic horror a fresh setting with a unique atmosphere
* Develop on Liminal Horror core ruleset for a rules-lite game system with a focus on non-punitive sanity mechanics, balancing narrative depth and faction-based choices to enhance replayability
* Design a comprehensive, well-organised product that includes: the narrative, Facilitator and Investigator rules, maps, and handouts.
* Available in both digital PDF and Paperback formats and as an optional hardback for collectors
